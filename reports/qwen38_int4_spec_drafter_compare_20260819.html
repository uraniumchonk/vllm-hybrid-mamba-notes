Notes

  • DFlash2 is the fastest drafter on structured output: numeric 237 (single) / 981 (n=8), code 195 / 618 — beats both MTP and DSpark.
  • Prose is DFlash2's weak spot (13.8% accept, 68.9 tok/s single) — same failure mode as DSpark but ~2x better. MTP still wins natural language.
  • DFlash2's acceptance profile is its killer feature: near-flat per-position decay on numeric (0.98 at pos6) vs DSpark's collapse (0.76/0.45/0.31/0.14/0.10/0.02/0.00).
  • Patches required (unmerged upstream): vLLM#52816 (DFlash2 drafter) + #52883 (unquantized LM head guard — required for AWQ-INT4's unquantized head) + #52460 (DSpark MRv2 align fallback).
  • LMCache was OFF for this run. DFlash2 + LMCache on 0.27.1 remains blocked by LMCache 0.5.3 SEGV (#4155).
  • MTP numbers are from 2026-08-19 (0.25.1, unique-prefix bench); DSpark from 2026-08-18 (0.27.1). Prompts identical across runs.
