Notes

  • FP8 wins prefill via INT8 tensor cores; loses decode because weights are 2.2x larger (31GB vs 14GB) — decode is bandwidth-bound.
  • On 20GB cards: ~15.5GB/card weights leave only ~1.4GB for KV cache (~85k tokens), so 128k context is impossible — dropped to 64k.
  • Vision works out of the box (tested with image input).
  • Eager to retest on 4x 24GB cards — decode and context should improve massively.
