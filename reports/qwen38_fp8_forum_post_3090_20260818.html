Notes

  • P2P does not move DSpark. Numeric 214 vs 206 is noise. This path is draft-compute bound, not card-to-card.
  • DSpark k=7 only pays on predictable output. Chinese prose is slower than turning DSpark off (43 vs 49).
  • Everyday chat / mixed Chinese still belongs to INT4 MTP on this box.
  • DSpark VRAM is already tight: 23909 / 24576 MiB used, ~215 MiB free. Do not raise gpu_memory_utilization. The draft model is what ate the native 327k KV down to 117k.
  • Native FP8 prefill ladder was not re-run on 3090 this session; 3080 prefill numbers stay in the old post.
